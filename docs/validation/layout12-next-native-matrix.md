The iOS `testSignalsRemainReachableInBothOrientations` already implements that value oracle. It terminates/relaunches between orientations and therefore cannot establish same-process resize retention.

For viewport invariance, use a small public `.circ` fixture with two distinct visible components whose world positions and drawing bounds are known from the exported project. Use public Fit and zoom controls to enter a manually positioned view, then resize/rotate without Fit, canvas gestures or selection changes. Retain the same two component identities and record their drawn centers relative to the measured canvas before and after. Their separation determines the observed scale; their known world positions determine pan and world center. Both scale and world center must agree within a documented pixel tolerance. Native semantic target bounds may be expanded for touch, so they must not silently substitute for drawn geometry. This two-anchor geometry oracle still needs implementation and execution; the existing single-point iOS case is useful narrower evidence.

The general native `checkFrame` default currently accepts 44 pt of intersection with the app window. It does not alone prove a whole 48 pt action inside safe areas. The next matrix must capture the relevant safe rectangle/insets and check complete action containment.
